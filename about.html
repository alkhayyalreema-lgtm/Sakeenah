About Sakeenah

Sakeenah is an Islamic website designed to help users discover Duas based on their emotions and explore daily Adhkar and Sunnah practices.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make Islamic remembrance easier to access and encourage users to include Duas and Adhkar in their daily lives.

What We Offer

  • Duas based on emotions
  • Daily Adhkar
  • Sunnah practices
  • A simple and peaceful experience
